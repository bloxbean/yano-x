Write a topics change

Fill in who owns a namespace, who may write to it and what its messages look like. Studio writes the topics.yaml the CLI plans, signs and submits.

Studio has no backend, never reads a chain and never asks for a key. The CLI checks the change against the chain and shows exactly what will be signed.

Fill in the form, or import a topics.yaml to edit.

The change

governance is a member vote (top-level namespaces and recovery); an owning namespace such as games/ is its owners' signatures; ~registrars and ~claim follow the chain's creation policy; ~issuer revokes your own credential.

Use the credential's exact scope as the target.
